About this project

Start with people's needs.

This short guide looks at positive applications of computing. Each story connects a practical example with official sources and considers what responsible design needs to address.

  1. 01

    Make the benefit concrete

    Explain the task a service helps with and who can benefit from it.

  2. 02

    Consider access and inclusion

    Think about different abilities, devices and routes to getting help.

  3. 03

    Keep the evidence in view

    Link to primary sources and distinguish examples from reported results.
