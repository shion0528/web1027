# I. Interest of Content
  Overall I found the website to be pretty interesting (and interactive!) to read through!
It covered quite a wide range of areas (environment, health, accessibility etc.) that computing
has attempted to make good across the past few decades in a *somewhat* detailed manner, although a few more case studies would be nice, haha.

# II. Consistency and Quality of Visual Presentation
  The website design and layout overall was very consistent across the entire website (probably
thanks to having a central styling document), and was overall very easy to navigate through,
apart from a few issues with the header clipping on the vscode integrated browser

# III. Quality of Written Presentation
  The overall writing was good, if not a bit AI-like, but overall it's still comprehensible and
provides a decent amount of information about each topic in the subpages.

# IV. Validity of HTML
  For accessibility and environment.html, those HTML pages were alright to review and was overall   
very well done in terms of structure and cohesion. For health and index.html however, the lack of indentation did make it a bit of an issue to review (as it was impossible to parse!)

# V. Evidence of Collaboration
  Definitely evidence of collaboration here as there were different authors for each of the pages, and a difference in HTML structure between accessibility/environment and health/index
